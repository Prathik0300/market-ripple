Episode library

How the map works. Each link has a sign (same or opposite direction), a weight from 0 to 1 and a typical lag. A shock is passed along up to four links; each hop multiplies the weights and loses 15%. Where two paths disagree (oil pushes gold up through inflation but down through higher yields) the item is flagged as mixed.

Measured links and the live desk. A local pipeline downloads about 21 years of daily prices for 73 market series plus the daily Geopolitical Risk index. For each link it finds days when the source moved more than two standard deviations and checks the target over the next 5 and 20 trading days against the target’s own base rate. “Link exists” includes the shock day; “predicts” starts the day after, which is what you could act on. A job every two hours logs new world events and refreshes these numbers.

Sources. 2026 episodes link to the reporting used in the October 2026 research. Earlier episodes are marked “approx.”: figures are rounded from the historical record and worth checking before you rely on them. Data as of 14 October 2026.
