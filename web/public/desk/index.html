How to read this desk

Score is how hard the last 14 days of events push the asset through its measured links. 100 is the strongest push on the list.

The three dots show whether the other evidence points the same way as the news: the model (only where it beat a naive forecast on unseen years), analysts (mostly buy for a tailwind, few buys for a headwind) and the price trend (1-month move and 200-day average). A filled dot agrees, a hollow one disagrees, a dotted one has no data.

Your portfolio

Your Robinhood, Groww, CoinDCX and mutual fund holdings, visible only to you: what each one moves with, what moved it, and how today’s news presses on it.

Connect investing platformsTrades and eventsConnectorsprivate
How to read this desk

News now is how hard the last 14 days of events push each holding through its measured links: below zero means the news leans against it.

Moves with numbers are correlations of weekly returns over three years: 1 means it moves in step, 0 means no relation. Indian stocks are measured against the Nifty 50.

Groww, CoinDCX and mutual fund amounts are in rupees; totals and weights count them in dollars at the day’s exchange rate. Mutual funds come from your CAMS statement and are valued at each day’s official NAV.

The model’s odds come from yearly retraining scored only on unseen years. None of your holdings beat a naive “rises about as often as usual” guess yet, so read them as close to a coin flip. This desk describes exposure; it does not tell you what to buy or sell.

How to read this desk

Each stock’s weekly moves are cleaned of its market, its sector and its size and style, then tested against 38 factors.

Robust links are very unlikely to be luck; likely ones are probably real. Both must keep their sign in all three thirds of the sample. Hidden marks a link the stock’s sector would not normally have.

Correlation tells you what moves together; only lead-lag tells you what comes next.

What each desk is for

Market Ripple traces world events to the assets they move. Each desk looks at that from one angle.

Everything here is research: it measures exposure and tests the model honestly. It is not advice to buy or sell.
